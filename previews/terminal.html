/* ──[ 05 · FAQ ]────────────────────────────────────────────────── */

does odissey provide movies or tv channels?▸
No. Odissey is software for media you are already authorized to use. It provides no content, no IPTV service, and no subscriptions to third-party libraries.
where does my media stay?▸
Exactly where it is today — your disks, your S3-compatible storage, your WebDAV shares. Nothing is uploaded.
what is included in the free version?▸
Everything. The self-hosted edition is the full product — catalog, profiles, metadata, live TV, playback — at $0 forever.
when will odissey hosted launch?▸
Hosted is in active development and will open from $10/month. Self-hosted remains free and fully featured either way.
can household members have separate profiles?▸
Yes. Each person gets independent progress, favorites and history.
